Strong foundations for literacy, numeracy, curiosity and character.

Summit Complex Nursery & Primary School provides a structured foundation for literacy, numeracy, curiosity, confidence and character across the formative years.

Age / entry: 3–11 years
Classes: Nursery 1–3 · Class 1–6
Daily schedule: 7:30 AM–3:00 PM, Monday–Friday

Our mission

Purpose for this division.

Build strong academic and personal foundations so learners develop confidence, good habits, curiosity, communication skills and the knowledge needed for the next stage of education.

Learning objective

Confident learners

Build knowledge, confidence and independence appropriate to the division's stage of education.

Personal development

Character & responsibility

Use everyday school life to strengthen discipline, teamwork, communication and responsible behaviour.

Progression

Ready for the next stage

Ensure learners leave the division with the academic and practical foundations needed for their next pathway.

Curriculum & programme

What learners study.

English language, mathematics, science, social studies, ICT, creative arts, physical education, moral education and age-appropriate project work.

01

Core learning

English language, mathematics, science, social studies, ICT, creative arts, physical education, moral education and age-appropriate project work.

02

Applied learning

Project work, problem-solving, practical exercises, collaborative tasks and classroom activities can be used to connect knowledge to real situations.

03

Assessment

Regular classwork, tests, assignments, projects and termly examinations can be used to monitor progress and guide support.
