Eligibility & documents

What families should prepare.

Different divisions may require different documents and placement conditions.

Daycare

Early childhood entry

  • Birth certificate or equivalent record
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Parent / guardian identification
  • Emergency and health information
  • Completed daycare enrollment form
Nursery & Primary

Basic education entry

  • Birth certificate
  • Transfer or previous school record where applicable
  • Passport photographs
  • Parent / guardian contact details
  • Placement assessment where required
Secondary / College

Post-primary entry

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school results or report
  • Transfer certificate where applicable
  • Passport photographs
  • Programme or class selection information

Admissions questions

What parents usually want to know.

Common questions about applying, placement, documents and registration.

Question 01

Can a learner transfer into Summit during the school year?

Transfers can be considered subject to available space, documentation, academic level and the school's approval process.

Question 02

Is there an entrance assessment?

Placement tests or interviews may be used for selected classes and programmes. Confirm requirements with admissions.

Question 03

How do I get the current fees?

Contact the admissions office for the latest approved fee structure and official payment instructions.
