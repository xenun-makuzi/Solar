Our story

A growing institution with a broad educational mandate.

Summit Complex was established in 2008 with the aim of providing accessible, structured and forward-looking education for children, adolescents and young adults in Bamenda.

The institution developed into a broader educational community with early childhood care, nursery and primary education, secondary grammar education, commercial studies and industrial technical training.

Mission

Develop capable, disciplined and confident learners.

To provide quality education that combines academic knowledge, practical skills, good character and responsible citizenship.

Vision

Prepare learners for further study, work and service.

To be a respected educational community where learners are equipped to succeed academically, professionally and personally.

Philosophy

Teach the whole learner.

Strong fundamentals, purposeful discipline, practical learning, curiosity, inclusion and preparation for real life.

Core values

What Summit stands for.

The Summit community is guided by values that shape learning, conduct, relationships and service.

01

02
